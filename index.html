Settings & Sync

Why your phone and laptop were different

The previous version stored data in browser storage. Your laptop and phone have separate browser storage, so changing a lesson on one device did not change the other. This version makes saving/editing safer, but true automatic syncing still requires a cloud database.

Move the latest data between devices

Use Backup on the device with the correct data, then Restore on the other device. This transfers your complete planner.
